State the remediation plan in every theory-adjacent SOP. Impact: converts "unaware of the gap" into "closing the gap" — the difference between rejection and a chance.
3. **GRE (hard gate at Stanford, Berkeley, Stevens; average Q168 at Booth).** Action: one sitting by early October, Q170 target. Bounded, known cost.

5. **Economics/econometrics depth.** Explicitly NOT worth remediating this cycle — it rules out Booth-style committees regardless; the portfolio already routes around it.

## E. Honest portfolio assessment

**Composition: 7 REACH, 2 TARGET, 1 LIKELY.** That is reach-heavy by any standard advice, and it is still the right shape for THIS candidate, for two reasons. First, the two largest quality upgrades (arXiv preprint, measure-theory evidence) act on all seven reaches at once between now and December. Second, the floor is unusually solid: Stevens is a near-lock admit where the real risk is funding, not admission — and the funding conversation is happening in September, months before any acceptance decision.

**Realistic expectations, stated plainly.** With the preprint done and GRE at target: expect 0-2 admits from the seven reaches (each is individually a 5-20% event for an unpublished applicant, even a well-matched one; they are partially correlated through the shared publication gap). UW is a genuine coin-flip-or-better TARGET. Stevens admission is highly likely; funded-on-acceptable-terms is the open question. Median outcome: 2-3 admits including Stevens; good outcome: a funded reach (most plausibly Berkeley via Mastrolia, Stanford via Pelger, or CBS DRO via Moallemi — the three where a specific professor's active agenda needs this specific skill set).

**What to cut:** CMU Math unless the measure-theory course is actually enrolled by October — an application without that evidence is a wasted fee. Booth stays cut. MIT EECS stays cut.

**What NOT to add:** more safeties. A second safety below Stevens serves no goal — if the cycle fails, the fallback is not a worse PhD, it is another year of industry with a published paper and a stronger reapplication (the candidate's stated goal is the QR/QD seat, and Phase 1 already established a PhD is optional for the QD path).

**The one asymmetry to respect:** the supervisor-first conversations (Mastrolia, Moallemi, Stellato replies, Florescu/Yang funding) will produce information by mid-October. If two or more of those turn warm, the reach-heavy shape is validated; if all go cold, add NYU Tandon ECE/FRE (mechanics-favorable backup) before its Dec 1 deadline and treat Stevens funding as the campaign's center of gravity.

**Immigration note (standing factor):** every US admit preserves the F-1 pathway and the EB-1B optionality through research output; the arXiv preprint serves that goal independently of admissions. No program on this list is dominated on immigration grounds.

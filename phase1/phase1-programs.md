## Headline findings

1. **The strongest placement-verified program is Chicago Booth (Econometrics & Statistics)**: named QR placements at Citadel, Citadel Securities, Chicago Trading Company, Point72, Squarepoint, Voleon on the official outcomes page. It is also a ~20-25-admit lottery with a hard GRE requirement.
2. **The best faculty-fit programs for the FPGA/market-making thesis**: Berkeley IEOR (Thibaut Mastrolia — market microstructure/market making, assistant professor building a group), Columbia IEOR (Agostino Capponi), IP Paris/CMAP (Mathieu Rosenbaum), LPSM/Université Paris Cité (Olivier Guéant — **explicitly accepting students; listed topics include market making and market impact**), Oxford (Álvaro Cartea), UW CFRM (Tim Leung).
3. **Track 2 is confirmed weak-ROI**: no US architecture group has an active FPGA-for-trading agenda; HFT hardware roles hire at BS/MS level. Keep at most MIT EECS (Alizadeh, low-latency networking) as a single Track 2 ticket; everything else on that side is a skip.
4. **A GRE sitting is unavoidable**: Booth, Stanford MS&E, Berkeley IEOR, Stevens FE, CMU Tepper, EPFL/SFI, and VGSF Vienna all require it. Take it by October 2026.
5. **Faculty-status catches that would have burned outreach emails**: Guéant moved Paris 1 → Université Paris Cité; Shkolnikov moved Princeton → CMU; Derek Chiou (UT Austin) is deceased; Alexandros Daglis (GT) is on leave in Edinburgh; David Thomas (FPGA finance) left Imperial for Southampton; Robert Kohn (NYU) is emeritus; Sasha Stoikov (Cornell) is research staff, not tenure-track; Svitlana Vyetrenko (Berkeley) is a lecturer.
6. **The publication gap binds hardest at every REACH**: getting the FPGA thesis onto arXiv and into a venue pipeline before December 2026 is worth more than any additional application.

## Cross-cutting strategy notes

**GRE plan.** Required at: Booth, Stanford MS&E, Berkeley IEOR, Stevens FE, EPFL/SFI, VGSF (+Tepper, skipped). Optional/absent everywhere else. One sitting by early October 2026 covers everything; Booth's admitted-average Quant is 168 — for this profile Q170 should be the target, and the verbal/AWA bar is low-stakes.

**Application mechanics split.** US/UK cohort deadlines cluster Dec 1 - Jan 15. The French/Swiss/Dutch supervisor-first track (Rosenbaum, Guéant, Teichmann, Menkveld, Luk) runs on email, not portals — those conversations should start in September-October 2026 and are effectively free options. Guéant's page explicitly invites CV + statement.

**The BNP Paribas card.** Rosenbaum and Guéant both have BNP-funded research history (Guéant's market-making work was BNP/Europlace-funded; Rosenbaum has a BNP co-author network). The 2026 BNP AMM co-op is the single strongest connector for the Paris track — arguably stronger than for any US program.

**Portfolio shape check (Phase 5 preview).** Apply list: ~10 REACH / ~13 TARGET / 1 LIKELY + 4 conditional. That is reach-heavy. The correction is not more safeties — it is (a) the supervisor-first European track, where fit substitutes for cohort competition, and (b) the arXiv preprint, which upgrades every REACH simultaneously. A realistic full campaign: 8-12 portal applications + 6-8 supervisor emails.

**Immigration note.** The salaried European models (ETH, NL, DK) come with employer-sponsored work authorization and no F-1/OPT dependency; a US PhD keeps the F-1 clock alive and preserves the EB-1B path via research output. Both routes serve the stated immigration goal; the US route serves the US-quant-placement goal more directly.
